# Hyper3D — Image-to-3D AI Generator
- **Workflow**:
  1. Upload single/multi-view images
  2. AI reconstructs geometry + PBR textures
  3. Export: GLB, FBX, OBJ, STL, USDZ, glTF, 3MF
- **Quality**: Gen-2.5 → 10M+ polygons, clean watertight topology
- **Use Cases**: Game dev, product design, AR/VR, 3D printing, art
- **URL**: https://hyper3d.ai/features/image-to-3D
