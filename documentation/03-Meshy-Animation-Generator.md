# Meshy — AI 3D Animation Generator
- **Core Feature**: Animate 3D characters with 600+ motion presets (walk, run, jump, attack, dance, combat, etc.)
- **Workflow**:
  1. Upload or generate 3D model
  2. Choose animation preset → preview live

- **Auto-Rigging**: Automatic skeleton/weight mapping
- **Exports**: Unity, Unreal, Blender, Godot, Maya, 3ds Max
